About
Mark

Mark Melara is a WGA writer and creative producer whose work spans television, gaming, and branded entertainment.

He broke into television through the sitcom world, with his first produced script directed by legendary television director James Burrows.

His work tends to live somewhere between the funny and the unsettling — stories with a distinct voice, a sense of humor, and something underneath worth digging into.
