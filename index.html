About
Mark

Mark Melara is a WGA writer, creative producer, and strategist whose work spans television, gaming, branded entertainment, and digital content.

He broke into television through the sitcom world, with his first produced script directed by legendary television director James Burrows.

His work is driven by voice, character, and a sense of humor, with an instinct for the deeper human truth underneath.
