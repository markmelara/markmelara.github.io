The Workshop

Build the series. Write the pilot.

We start with the idea you want to make into a television show and pressure-test the pieces that have to hold: premise, character, world, tone, engine, and structure. From there, the class moves into breaking and drafting the pilot, with live discussion and direct feedback throughout.

The goal is a stronger series concept, a clearer plan for the pilot, and pages that reflect what makes your project distinct.

Eight Weeks
Week 01

Premise & Series Engine

Clarify what the show is, what keeps generating story, and why this version is worth making.

Week 02

Character

Build characters with wants, contradictions, relationships, and enough pressure to sustain a series.

Week 03

World & Tone

Define the rules of the world and the tonal lane the show needs to live in.

Week 04

Pilot Story

Find the story that best introduces the series and forces the central characters into motion.

Week 05

Structure & Outline

Break the episode, test turns and escalation, and build an outline you can actually draft from.

Week 06

Drafting

Move into pages with a focus on scene purpose, dialogue, pace, and character behavior.

Week 07

Workshop & Revision

Diagnose what the draft is doing, where it drifts, and what changes will produce the biggest gains.

Week 08

The Next Draft

Leave with a concrete revision plan for the pilot and a clearer understanding of the series beyond it.

Who It's For

Writers ready to work.

You have a series idea and need help shaping it into a workable television concept.
You have a pilot in progress and want stronger structure, character, and story choices.
You want direct feedback from a working television writer and a small group of serious writers.
You are prepared to write, revise, and bring pages into the room.
